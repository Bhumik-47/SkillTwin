# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **SkillTwin** project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and inclusive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Expected Behavior

We encourage all contributors and participants to:
- **Demonstrate empathy and kindness** toward other people.
- **Be respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- **Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback**.
- **Accept responsibility and apologize** to those affected by our mistakes, learning from the experience.
- **Focus on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall SkillTwin project and community.
- **Collaborate transparently** and support fellow teammates, especially in fast-paced hackathon and open-source environments.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include, but are not limited to:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.
- Intentionally submitting malicious code, unverified security vulnerabilities, or secrets into the repository.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (including the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and associated communication channels), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

## Reporting Process

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the repository maintainers through private GitHub communication channels or by opening a confidential discussion with the repository administrators.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
